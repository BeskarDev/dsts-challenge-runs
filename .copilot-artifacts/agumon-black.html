In-game description

A small, dinosaur-like Reptile Digimon that has developed the ability to walk on two legs. Since it hasn't fully matured, it still lacks strength, but its fierce disposition makes it reckless. Hard, sharp claws grow from its hands and feet, and it makes full use of them in battle. This Digimon shows much potential, promising Digivolution into a great and powerful Digimon. Its special move is Spitfire Blast.
